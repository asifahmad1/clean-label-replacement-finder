Swap synthetic additives for natural, consumer-friendly alternatives — with dosages, trade-offs and label guidance.

Honest scope: “Clean label” is a consumer and marketing concept, not a legal category. Natural replacements may themselves carry E-numbers — what changes is the declaration and perception, not always the chemistry. Preservative and antioxidant swaps must be proven in challenge tests and shelf-life studies before launch. Dosages are indicative starting points for trials, never guarantees. Regulatory status varies by market and changes over time — confirm the current rules for your product category before printing a label.
